# Racing sounds

Replaces the old player engine sine tone and repeated boost/skid beeps with:
- Three original synthesized engine voices: heavy (Bulwark/Nomad), exotic
  (Phantom/Apex), and sport (Viper/Comet).
- Smoothed revs responding to speed and throttle, six simulated sound gears,
  rev drops and shift transients. These audio gears do not change vehicle physics.
- Continuous tyre squeal when drifting, boost roar, and player speed-dependent wind.
- Collision thumps scaled by impact velocity with a repeat cooldown.
- Quieter spatial rival engines and pass-by Doppler.

Master Volume and Effects Volume control the mix. Race audio stops during pause,
resume countdown, loss of focus and results. It does not play in the showroom.
The initial starting grid has idle engines. Music and existing race signals remain.

All eight WAV assets are original synthesis, generated by
`python Tools/Audio/generate_racing_sounds.py`, with no third-party samples.
These are stylized arcade effects, not recordings of real engines. Short effects
use preloaded PCM rather than streaming. Shared imported clips are not destroyed
by individual cars.

Checked in cloud: waveform sample bounds, durations, source syntax and references.
Added Unity PlayMode coverage for imported clips, player/rival audio, pause/resume
and Effects Volume mute. Unity execution and listening/balance testing on speakers
and headphones remain required on Windows; no Windows build was made here.
