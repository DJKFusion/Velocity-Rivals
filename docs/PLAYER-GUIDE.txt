VELOCITY RIVALS — SINGLE PLAYER

GETTING STARTED
Extract the entire game ZIP. Keep VelocityRivals.exe, UnityPlayer.dll and the
VelocityRivals_Data folder together, then launch VelocityRivals.exe.
No account or online server is needed to play.

Choose DRIVING SCHOOL to practise speed, boost, jumping and recovery in Cyber Dome.
Complete the four actions to save your training completion and enter the career.
PLAY offers unrestricted Free Play as well as timed and competitive events.
Event selection shows the course outline, start marker and selected car.
CAREER TOUR contains 12 events across four cups. Bronze opens the next event.
TOUR PROGRESS displays your earned medals and locked events. Replay for gold.
Championships have three rounds; use NEXT CHAMPIONSHIP ROUND after each result.
Complete the tour to reach the finale. All gold medals earn another achievement.
Failed events offer TRY AGAIN. A career bronze medal counts as event completion.
Leaving the track returns your car to safety without skipping checkpoints; the
event clock keeps running. R / Y also recovers you if stuck or facing the wrong way.

GARAGE AND CREDITS
Finish events to earn credits. Improved career medals earn a one-time bonus.
The dealership lets you buy the next car early; free XP unlocks still work.
The performance workshop offers Engine, Handling and Boost upgrades for each car.
Stages cost 150, 300 and 450 credits. Time Attack always uses stock performance.
Purchases need confirmation and are saved automatically. Existing cars stay owned.

CONTROLS
Keyboard: W/S accelerate/reverse, A/D steer, Shift boost, Ctrl handbrake,
Space jump (press again for an aerial jump), Q/E air roll, R recover, Esc pause.
Controller: triggers accelerate/reverse, left stick steer, B boost, X handbrake,
A jump, LB plus stick air roll, Y recover, Start pause.
Right stick looks around; click it (R3) or press C to centre the camera.
Camera sensitivity and inverted vertical look are available in SETTINGS.
The camera returns behind your car after you release the stick.
Menus: mouse, arrows or D-pad; Enter or A selects; Esc or B goes back.
Use the mouse wheel to scroll long pages. Keyboard/controller selection scrolls
into view automatically. Speed and boost appear in a separate driving display.

EVENTS
Illuminated recharge lanes on circuits restore 45 percent boost. Each lane has
a five-second cooldown per car. Choose your line to collect the recharge.
Championship: pass each checkpoint in order and finish three laps per round.
Time Attack: complete three laps; your best run becomes a personal ghost.
Arena Clash: push the core into the orange goal; first team to five wins.
Boost Battle: hit opponents at speed and collect combat power-ups.
Stunt Rush: jump, rotate and land upright; aim for at least 1,000 points.
Free Play: untimed practice. Pause to return to the menu.
Elimination: four cars start. Last place is removed every 45 seconds. Keep passing
checkpoints in order and survive three cutoffs to win. No fixed lap target.
The last ten seconds show a cutoff warning; your car is marked in danger if last.
Eliminated rivals disappear from the track and map. A loss offers retry; winning
earns event credits, XP and the Last survivor achievement.

SLIPSTREAM AND RIVALS
In Championship and Elimination, follow an aligned, moving car 5–30 metres ahead.
Stay within roughly one car width and above 45 km/h to build drafting strength.
The speed card shows DRAFT while active. Full strength adds up to 10 percent
unboosted top speed and 8 percent acceleration. Pull out to pass; strength fades.
Time Attack is solo, with no slipstream. Rival drivers also use slipstream.
Ion is cautious, Prism uses boost more aggressively, and Comet is balanced.
Rivals check adjacent lanes before passing and slow for bends and blocked traffic.

PROGRESS
Events award XP; each 500 XP unlocks another vehicle until all six are available.
Garage selections, settings, records and career medals save on this computer.
Leaving an unfinished event does not award a career medal. Restarting a
championship resets its current rounds, but keeps previously saved progress.
Version 0.5 introduces new circuit layouts. Older circuit records remain labeled
as old layouts. New ghosts and times are stored separately. Unlocks are retained.

DISPLAY AND COMFORT
SETTINGS > DISPLAY & EFFECTS offers Performance, Balanced and Quality presets,
windowed/borderless display, and Reduced effects. Reduce quality if driving is
not smooth. Reduced effects disables bloom, vignette, impact bursts, drift smoke,
showroom rotation and speed-related camera widening.

SAVES AND TROUBLESHOOTING
Windows save folder: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\DJKFusion\Velocity Rivals
The profile is velocity-profile.json; its .bak file contains an earlier save.
Back up this folder before moving PCs or changing save files.
If the game fails, retain Player.log from the same folder and note the version,
event, environment, selected car and what happened immediately beforehand.
An on-screen save error means your latest progress may not be on disk.
